Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Encore Cinema is made by Encore Audio Ltd ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17539933), registered office 128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX. We are responsible for your data. This policy covers the Encore Cinema TV app, Encore Cinema Server and this website. Questions: email contact@encoreaudio.co.uk.

The short version

  • The Encore Cinema app and Encore Cinema Server send nothing to us. Your films, what you watch and your settings stay in your home.
  • There are no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking in the app.
  • This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking, and no adverts.
  • If you register your interest, we use your name and email only to tell you about Encore Cinema. We never sell or share them.
  • You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

The Encore Cinema app

The app on your TV talks only to the computers in your home that run Encore Cinema Server or Encore Audio. It finds them on your home network and pairs with them using a 6-digit code. Pairing stores a key on your TV and on your PC, so they recognise each other. The app sends your PC your TV's model name (so you can tell your TVs apart), what you're watching and how far through you are (for "Continue watching"). All of this stays on your own PC.

The app doesn't collect personal data, doesn't contact our servers, and has no accounts, adverts, analytics or crash reporting. If you play internet radio through it, your TV fetches the stream (and the station's logo) straight from the station you choose, as any radio player does.

Encore Cinema Server

The server runs on your own computer and keeps its library, settings and paired TVs there. If you give it a TMDB key, it asks TMDB (The Movie Database) for posters and details of your films, sending the film titles and years it finds in your folders; see TMDB's privacy policy. Your beta key is checked on your computer, without contacting us.

This website

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, records technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site; see GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing else is loaded from other sites.

Registering your interest

The form asks for your name, email address, what you watch on and whether you'd like to be a beta tester. It's delivered by Formspree, a form service that passes what you write on to us by email and stores it for a limited time to do so; see Formspree's privacy policy.

We use your answers to tell you when the Encore Cinema beta opens and when it's released, and to choose beta testers. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by replying to any of our emails or writing to us. We keep your details until you ask us to remove them, or until Encore Cinema is released and we've let you know, whichever is sooner.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email contact@encoreaudio.co.uk. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes
