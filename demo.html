Try the demo

Want to see Encore Cinema before setting anything up at home? The TV app has a demo built in. It connects to our server over the internet, with three free films and a music library to play on your TV. No PC and no account needed.

How to start it

  1. Install Encore Cinema on your Google TV, Android TV or Fire TV. The app is in testing on Google Play and will be available to everyone soon.
  2. Open it and choose "Try the demo" on the first screen, under "No Encore yet?".
  3. Pick a film and press Play. It streams to your TV in its original quality, so a good internet connection helps, especially for the 4K film.

To leave the demo, go to Settings and choose Leave the demo. You can then set up Encore Cinema Server and Encore Audio on your own PC.

What you can try

4K at 60 frames a second

Big Buck Bunny in Ultra HD, with 5.1 surround sound.

Subtitles

Ten languages built into Sintel, and nine as separate subtitle files for Tears of Steel.

Versions

Tears of Steel comes in stereo and 5.1 surround versions: choose either from the film's page.

Your library, browsed

Posters, backdrops, film details from Kodi .nfo files, and Continue watching.

Music too

The Encore Audio demo library plays on your TV as well: hi-res classical, jazz and more.

Your sound system

Surround sound goes to your soundbar or AV receiver just as it would from your own films.

The demo shows the app, not your own films. Dolby Vision and lossless Dolby Atmos need films that have them, so you'll see those with your own collection on Encore Cinema Server. The demo is shared by everyone trying it, so "Continue watching" may show films other people have started.
