Lossless Dolby Atmos

How it works

  1. Install Encore Cinema Server on a Windows PC
    Point it at your film and TV folders, on the PC or on a NAS. It reads what's there, including any artwork and details you already keep beside your films.
  2. Install the Encore Cinema app on your TV
    Google TV, Android TV (such as Nvidia Shield) or Amazon Fire TV.
  3. Pair with a code, and press play
    The TV finds your PC on your home network. Enter the code it shows, and your whole collection is there.

Got Encore Audio too? The same TV app plays your music library, bit-perfect, with your films and TV. Music you were playing on your phone or in another room is waiting on the TV's home screen, ready to carry on from the same second. Encore Cinema also works perfectly well on its own. Questions

When can I get it, and what will it cost?

Encore Cinema is in development now, and we'll open a beta first. Prices aren't decided yet. Register your interest below and we'll tell you as soon as you can try it.

Does it change or compress my films?

No. It never transcodes: your TV box plays the original file. That means the box needs to support the format, which most modern 4K streaming boxes do for HEVC, Dolby Vision and HDR10. The one thing it may do is decode a soundtrack your sound system can't take as it is, such as DTS-HD on a Sonos soundbar, into lossless surround on the box. Nothing is lost.

Will I hear Dolby Atmos?

If your film has a Dolby TrueHD Atmos track and your streaming box can pass it through to an Atmos receiver or soundbar, yes: Encore Cinema sends it untouched. We've tested this with an Amazon Fire TV Cube through an LG TV to a Sonos Arc Ultra.

Will DTS-HD play in full on my Sonos?

Yes. Sonos soundbars only play the lossy part of a DTS soundtrack, so Encore Cinema decodes DTS-HD Master Audio itself, on your TV box, and sends the soundbar lossless 5.1 or 7.1 surround (multichannel PCM). This needs eARC between your TV and the soundbar. With DTS:X, the full 7.1 comes through but the height effects don't. It switches on automatically when your TV can't take DTS, and there's a setting for TVs that pass DTS on to a soundbar that can't play it in full. We've tested this with a Fire TV Cube, an LG TV and a Sonos Arc Ultra.

Which TVs and boxes does it work with?

It's made for Google TV and Android TV devices and Amazon Fire TV. During the beta we'll be testing on as many of them as we can. A phone app is planned.

Where do the posters and film details come from?

Artwork and details saved beside your films come first, in the common Kodi format. Anything missing can be looked up online.

Do I need Encore Audio?

No. Encore Cinema works on its own. If you also have Encore Audio, one app on your TV gives you films, TV and music.
